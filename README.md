# Virtual-Tableau-Visualization-Intern
Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis and Insight Generation
In this task, I used the Online Retail dataset and performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using Tableau Public.
Visualizations Created
- Monthly Sales Trend – Shows how sales changed over time and helps identify high- and low-sales months.
- Sales by Country – Compares total sales across different countries and identifies the highest-performing markets.
- Top 10 Products – Shows the products generating the highest sales.
- Quantity vs Sales – Shows the relationship between the quantity sold and total sales.
- Top 10 Customers by Sales – Identifies customers who contribute the most to sales.
- Sales by Month and Country Heatmap – Shows sales patterns across different countries and months.
- Outlier Analysis – Uses the Quantity vs Sales relationship to identify unusual transactions and extreme values.
- Business KPIs – Summarizes important metrics such as total sales, quantity, customers, and orders.
- Interactive EDA Dashboard – Combines the major visualizations into one dashboard for easy analysis.
-Tools Used: Tableau Public, Microsoft Excel.
